# Hangman Game Play Guide

## How to Play Hangman

Hangman is a classic word-guessing game where players try to guess a hidden word by suggesting letters. The game ends when the player either guesses the entire word correctly or fails to guess it within the allowed number of incorrect attempts.

### Step-by-Step Gameplay

1. **Word Display**: A random word is selected and displayed as a series of blanks (underscores) representing each letter in the word.
2. **Guessing Letters**: The player suggests a letter they believe might be in the hidden word.
3. **Feedback**:
   - If the letter is in the word, it is revealed in its correct position(s).
   - If the letter is not in the word, a part of the hangman is drawn, indicating an incorrect guess.
4. **Continue Guessing**: The player continues guessing letters until one of the following occurs:
   - The entire word is guessed correctly (the player wins).
   - The hangman is fully drawn (the player loses).

### Example Gameplay

#### Scenario 1: Correct Guess
- Hidden word: `hello`
- Initial display: `_ _ _ _ _`
- Player guesses 'e'
- Display updates to: `_ e _ _ _`
- Player continues guessing until the full word is revealed.

#### Scenario 2: Incorrect Guess
- Hidden word: `world`
- Initial display: `_ _ _ _ _`
- Player guesses 'x'
- A part of the hangman is drawn (incorrect guess)
- Player continues guessing letters.

### Tips for Success

- Start with common vowels (a, e, i, o, u) as they appear frequently in English words.
- Pay attention to repeated letters and their positions.
- Keep track of guessed letters to avoid repeating incorrect guesses.

## Conclusion

Hangman is a fun and educational game that helps improve vocabulary and spelling skills. Enjoy the challenge!
